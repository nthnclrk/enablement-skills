## A versus B

**Prefer A for tomorrow's manager 1:1.** Both identify the right two priorities and are faithful to the excerpt. The practical difference is that A gives the manager a clearer distinction to teach: approval is a process step, while requirements and proof still need discovery. Its question asks directly how IT will check the integration, and its replay instructions help the manager run the practice. B's evidence-review guidance is usable, but the manager must make that validation question more explicit. This preference rests on coaching precision and rehearsal support, not artifact length.

# Release-check prompts

All companies, people and source material in these cases are fictional. Coaching and objection prompts were held out from the earlier trials. Each of four fresh drafting agents received both tasks; two loaded the relevant skill and two did not. The outputs were not supplied to later drafting agents.

## Coaching

Write feedback I can paste into our AE's coaching notes, 100–150 words. Keep it conversational; we use no named sales methodology and don't need scores. This is the only excerpt available:

- [06:10] Buyer: 'Supervisors copy the same shift change into three systems. We missed a cover request on Saturday.'
- [06:22] AE: 'What happened when that was missed?'
- [06:30] Buyer: 'The dispatch lead covered it herself and the outbound queue waited until Monday.'
- [06:48] AE: 'Who would need to test a replacement?'
- [06:56] Buyer: 'Dispatch and payroll. Payroll can't test until after month-end.'
- [07:08] AE: 'Our approvals screen should help. I'll send a demo and call you next Tuesday.'

Give the rep something useful to keep and one thing to practise next time.

## Objection

I'm about to speak to an operations buyer who said, 'The other supplier is cheaper and your rollout sounds like more work.' Help me reply in no more than 120 words. I have no competitor quote or rollout estimate. Our product supports a permissions-controlled audit trail. The buyer hasn't said whether that matters. I can't offer a discount or promise a go-live date. I need words I can say, not a playbook.

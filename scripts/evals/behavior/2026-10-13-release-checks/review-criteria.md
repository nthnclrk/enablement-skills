# Review criteria

Apply these criteria to the anonymous outputs and original prompts. Do not read the skills, condition mapping or earlier results. These criteria were recorded before independent grading; they were not supplied to the drafting agents.

Score evidence fidelity, enablement judgement, practical usability and proportionality from 0 to 3: 0 harmful or unusable; 1 major repair; 2 minor repair; 3 usable as delivered. Do not produce an overall release score.

For coaching, check that the response preserves the demonstrated consequence question, cites an accessible moment, proposes one relevant behaviour to practise, and fits 100–150 words. It must not invent tone, deal outcomes or evidence beyond the excerpt, score the rep, or impose a named methodology. Consider whether the proposed practice addresses the most useful gap; more impact discovery and agreeing a feasible test are both possible choices and need to be judged against the excerpt.

For objections, check that the response is speakable and no longer than 120 words; acknowledges the concern; asks a neutral question; and offers an appropriate next move. It must not verify the competitor's price, invent effort estimates or savings, promise a date, or offer a discount. If it mentions the audit trail, it must establish whether the buyer needs it. Omitting that capability is acceptable.

Report any failed constraints. Compare A/B/C/D within each task and explain preferences or ties. A single preference does not establish reliable superiority.

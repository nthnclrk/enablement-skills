# Audit Scope

All details below are fictional.

## Planning Frame

- As-of date: 2026-07-24
- Planning horizon: next 90 days
- Segment: Mid-market
- Roles: Account Executives and Sales Engineers
- Stages: Discovery, Evaluation, and Commercial

## Business Priorities

1. Improve discovery around measurable business impact.
2. Reduce avoidable security-review friction.
3. Give sellers credible value support during commercial discussions.

## Decision Rules

- Low usage alone is not sufficient evidence to retire an asset.
- Treat current approved content with high failed-search volume as a discoverability problem before assuming a content-quality problem.
- Treat a newer approved canonical replacement as evidence for consolidation, subject to owner review.
- Enterprise-only assets do not automatically satisfy mid-market coverage.
- Do not penalize draft assets for having no usage.

## Evidence Limits

- View data covers the previous 90 days only.
- Failed-search data comes from the enablement hub and does not include the legacy wiki.
- Field requests were coded manually and may contain duplicates.
- No direct quality audit or seller survey was provided.

## Output Decision

Return a small backlog that can realistically be completed or validated within 90 days. Recommend owners and validation steps, but do not modify content systems.

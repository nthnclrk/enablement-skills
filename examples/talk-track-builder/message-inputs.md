# Message Inputs

All details below are fictional and sanitized.

## Conversation Context

- Company: MosaicOps
- Product: workflow software for cross-team operational handoffs
- Seller: mid-market account executive
- Buyer: VP Operations at a 900-person distribution company
- Stage: first substantive discovery call after an inbound enquiry
- Time available: 12 minutes before a scheduled technical overview
- Call objective: determine whether missed handoffs create material delivery risk and secure a workflow-review session with Operations and IT

## Buyer Context

- Current approach: shared spreadsheets, email, and team chat
- Trigger: two recent delivery escalations exposed unclear ownership
- Likely concern: another workflow tool may add process overhead
- Likely objection 1: “We already have a handoff process.”
- Likely objection 2: “The team will not adopt another system.”
- Unknowns: economic impact, executive sponsor, IT integration requirements, and decision timing

## Approved Product Claims

- Captures handoff requests from email and supported team-chat workflows
- Assigns owners and alerts teams when agreed handoff windows are missed
- Reports unresolved handoffs and recurring bottlenecks
- Supports standard identity and team-chat integrations

## Approved Proof

- In a three-customer pilot, unresolved handoffs fell by 28% within 60 days.
- Treat the pilot result as directional; it is not an industry benchmark or a guaranteed customer outcome.
- Median deployment for the pilot customers was 21 days using standard integrations.
- MosaicOps has a current SOC 2 Type II report available through the security review process.

## Claim Boundaries

- Do not promise deployment timing for custom ERP integrations.
- Do not say MosaicOps eliminates delivery delays.
- Pricing and discount authority are not included in this brief.
- Route custom architecture questions to the solutions engineer.

## Desired Style

- Calm, concise, and curious
- Use buyer language rather than product terminology
- Prefer short prompts and optional follow-ups over long monologues

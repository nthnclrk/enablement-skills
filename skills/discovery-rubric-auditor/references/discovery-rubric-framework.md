# Discovery Rubric Framework

Use this framework to score discovery quality on a 1-5 scale.

## Scoring Scale

- `1`: behavior absent or actively harmful.
- `2`: inconsistent and low-quality execution.
- `3`: baseline quality with visible gaps.
- `4`: strong, repeatable execution.
- `5`: high-performing execution with strategic depth.

## Dimensions

### 1. Problem Depth

- Moves beyond surface pain into root causes.
- Connects issues to business impact, not only workflow friction.

### 2. Stakeholder Mapping

- Identifies decision roles, influencers, and blockers.
- Tests alignment and conflicting priorities early.

### 3. Current State And Process

- Clarifies how work gets done today and where failure occurs.
- Identifies constraints: systems, resources, or policy.

### 4. Impact And Urgency

- Quantifies or qualifies consequences of inaction.
- Surfaces timing drivers and competing initiatives.

### 5. Success Criteria

- Clarifies what outcome is required for buyer confidence.
- Defines evaluation criteria and proof expectations.

### 6. Next-Step Quality

- Secures concrete next step with owners and timeline.
- Advances deal clarity instead of generic follow-up.

## Aggregation Rules

- Use median score per dimension for cohort summaries.
- Flag any dimension below `3` as a coaching risk.
- Prioritize interventions where frequency and impact are both high.

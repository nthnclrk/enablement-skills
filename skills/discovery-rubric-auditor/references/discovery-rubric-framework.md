# Discovery audit judgment

Use this when you are scoring a sample or checking a pattern. The skill body has the procedure. One call belongs in `call-review-coach`.

## Pattern test

Keep a finding if it recurs in a comparable slice and a manager can inspect it next week. Cut it if it is one ugly call, a mixed leaderboard, or a cause you cannot show.

**Fails.** "The team is weak at discovery. AE3 scored 2.1."

**Passes.** "In 7 of 9 mid-market first calls this month, reps named a problem and never asked what it costs. Coach the cost question. Do not rank the AE with two calls."

## Score only what appeared

- `N/O`: not observable in the call or the available evidence.
- `1`: required behavior absent or harmful.
- `3`: baseline, with visible but non-fatal gaps.
- `5`: consistently strong, adaptive, strategic depth.
- Use `2` or `4` only between adjacent anchors.

Silence in missing audio is not a `1`. Platform scores are leads, not ground truth.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | `1` | `3` | `5` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Problem depth | Accepts surface pain or leads to an assumed problem. | Problem, relevant root cause, business consequence. | Follows signals through trade-offs and evidence without forcing a story. |
| Stakeholder mapping | One contact as the decision, or invented stakeholders. | Relevant roles, influence, basic alignment or gaps. | Tests authority, competing priorities, and blocker dynamics. |
| Current state and process | Workflow vague, or jumps to solution. | Process, failure points, material constraints. | Dependencies, exceptions, and why current approaches fail. |
| Impact and urgency | Unqualified urgency or manufactured pressure. | Consequence qualified or quantified, credible timing. | Impact, cost of delay, and a decision window with evidence. |
| Success criteria | Outcomes and evaluation criteria undefined. | Outcome and basic proof the buyer will use. | Measurable outcomes, decision criteria, trade-offs across stakeholders. |
| Next-step quality | Generic follow-up or seller-only actions. | Specific next step with owners and timing. | Mutual step tied to unresolved risk, evidence, and a success condition. |

## Calibration

Score two benchmark calls independently before the portfolio. Resolve every gap greater than one point. Rescore one benchmark. Double-score at least two live calls or 10% of the sample when practical.

One reviewer: score benchmarks first, blindly rescore a subset after a delay, report self-consistency, and label `inter-rater reliability: not established`. Do not imply a second rater existed.

**Fails.** "Reviewer agreement was strong" after one person scored the file.

**Passes.** "One reviewer. Delayed rescore: 8 of 10 dimensions within one point. Inter-rater reliability: not established. Aggregates are directional."

## Thin evidence

- Keep the denominator on every number.
- Compare only like role, segment, stage, purpose, and window, or show the groups separately.
- Report count, median, distribution, `N/O` rate, and coverage.
- Fewer than three comparable calls per reported unit is directional. Do not rank reps from that floor.
- Do not average `N/O` as zero. Do not turn frequency into causation.
- Default to de-identified reporting. Identifiable call notes stay restricted.

**Fails.** Averaging three `N/O` stakeholder scores into a team 1.8 on stakeholder mapping.

**Passes.** "Stakeholder mapping: 4 scored calls, median 3, 5 `N/O` because first-call recordings ended at 18 minutes. Directional only."

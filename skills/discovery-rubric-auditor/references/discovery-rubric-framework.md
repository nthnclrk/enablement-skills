# Discovery Rubric Framework

Use this framework to score comparable discovery-call samples. A single-call review belongs in `call-review-coach`.

## Scoring Scale

- `N/O`: behavior was not observable in the call or available evidence.
- `1`: required behavior is observably absent or actively harmful.
- `3`: behavior meets the baseline independently, with visible but non-fatal gaps.
- `5`: behavior is consistently strong, adaptive, and creates strategic depth.
- Use `2` or `4` only when evidence falls between adjacent anchors.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | `1` Anchor | `3` Anchor | `5` Anchor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Problem depth | Accepts surface pain or leads the buyer to an assumed problem. | Establishes the problem, relevant root cause, and business consequence to baseline depth. | Follows buyer signals through root cause, trade-offs, affected outcomes, and evidence without forcing a narrative. |
| Stakeholder mapping | Treats one contact as the decision system or invents stakeholder assumptions. | Identifies relevant roles, influence, and basic alignment or gaps. | Tests authority, competing priorities, blocker dynamics, and how stakeholder alignment affects the decision. |
| Current state and process | Leaves the workflow vague or jumps to solutioning before understanding it. | Clarifies the current process, failure points, and material constraints. | Maps process, dependencies, exceptions, and why previous or current approaches fail. |
| Impact and urgency | Accepts unqualified urgency or creates artificial pressure. | Qualifies or quantifies consequence and identifies a credible timing driver. | Connects impact, cost of delay, competing priorities, and timing to an evidence-based decision window. |
| Success criteria | Leaves desired outcomes or evaluation criteria undefined. | Establishes the outcome and basic proof the buyer will use to judge success. | Defines measurable outcomes, decision criteria, proof expectations, and material trade-offs across stakeholders. |
| Next-step quality | Ends with generic follow-up or seller-only actions. | Secures a specific next step with owners and timing. | Secures a mutual step tied to unresolved decision risk, required evidence, owners, and a clear success condition. |

## Rater Calibration

1. Select at least two representative benchmark calls that expose most dimensions.
2. Have reviewers score independently with evidence before discussing results.
3. Compare exact agreement, within-one-point agreement, and any critical interpretation differences.
4. Resolve every disagreement greater than one point and document the agreed anchor interpretation.
5. Rescore one benchmark before portfolio scoring. For high-stakes use, require stricter agreement and an independent governance process.
6. Double-score at least two calls or 10% of the live sample, whichever is greater when practical, and recalibrate if drift appears.

### Single-Reviewer Path

When only one reviewer is available:

1. Score known benchmark anchors before the live sample and record where judgment remains uncertain.
2. After a delay, blindly rescore a representative subset without seeing the first result.
3. Report exact and within-one-point self-consistency, adjudicate material differences against evidence, and record any anchor change.
4. Label `inter-rater reliability: not established`. Keep aggregation directional when self-consistency or evidence coverage is weak.

## Aggregation Rules

- Retain call-level scores and evidence; never report an aggregate without its denominator.
- Compare only calls with materially similar role, segment, stage, purpose, and time window, or show those groups separately.
- Report sample count, median, score distribution, `N/O` rate, and evidence coverage per dimension.
- Treat fewer than three comparable calls per reported unit as directional when no stronger sample rule was agreed. Do not rank reps or infer team prevalence from that floor.
- Prioritize interventions where frequency, business impact, evidence confidence, and coachability are all material.
- Do not average `N/O` as zero or imply causation from a recurring correlation.

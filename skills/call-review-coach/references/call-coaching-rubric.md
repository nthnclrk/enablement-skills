# Call Coaching Rubric

Use this rubric when reviewing individual calls. Score each dimension from 1 to 5.

## Scoring Scale

- `1`: Missing or harmful behavior.
- `2`: Inconsistent behavior with major quality gaps.
- `3`: Meets baseline expectation.
- `4`: Strong and repeatable behavior.
- `5`: High-performing behavior that can be modeled for others.

## Dimensions

### 1. Call Control And Agenda

- Sets a clear agenda and secures buy-in.
- Uses time intentionally and reaches expected checkpoints.
- Closes with clear next steps and ownership.

### 2. Discovery Quality

- Asks layered questions that move from symptoms to business impact.
- Validates current process, urgency, and consequences of inaction.
- Surfaces stakeholder context rather than single-contact detail.

### 3. Value Framing

- Connects product capability to customer outcomes.
- Uses customer language instead of feature-only description.
- Quantifies or qualifies impact where possible.

### 4. Objection Handling

- Acknowledges concern without defensiveness.
- Diagnoses the real concern before responding.
- Resolves or advances the conversation with a concrete next step.

### 5. Commercial Progression

- Clarifies decision process, timeline, and evaluation criteria.
- Confirms buying roles and approval path.
- Reduces deal ambiguity before ending the call.

### 6. Executive Communication

- Communicates concise, outcome-first messages.
- Avoids jargon and over-explaining.
- Maintains credibility under pushback.

## Coaching Translation Rules

- Keep only top 1-3 priorities per call.
- Convert each priority into one observable behavior.
- Set a checkpoint for the next call review.

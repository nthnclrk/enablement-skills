# Revenue Enablement Context Schema

Use this schema when creating or updating the canonical context artifact.

## 1) Business Snapshot

- Company
- Product lines
- Target market
- Revenue model

## 2) ICP And Segments

- Primary ICP definition
- Segment splits (company size, industry, geography)
- Segment exclusions

## 3) Buying Committee

For each core persona:

- Role and accountability
- Business pain
- Decision criteria
- Typical objections

## 4) Sales Motion

- Funnel stages and entry/exit criteria
- Qualification definition
- Core conversion risks by stage

## 5) Packaging And Commercials

- Packaging summary
- Pricing logic and major levers
- Discount or approval constraints

## 6) Messaging Baseline

- Positioning statement
- Core value pillars
- Proof points and evidence sources

## 7) Competitive Context

For top alternatives:

- Where they are strong
- Where you are strong
- Common traps and response guidance

## 8) Current Initiatives

- Current-quarter priorities
- Launches or strategic changes
- Enablement dependencies and deadlines

## 9) Governance

- Source of truth location
- Document owner
- Last-updated date
- Open assumptions and unknowns

# Case study judgment

Use this when extracting or checking proof. The skill body has the procedure.

## Permission is not evidence

A verified metric can still be internal-only. An approved logo does not make a causal claim true.

Statuses for evidence: `Verified`, `Supported`, `Anecdotal`, `Unverified`.
Statuses for use: `Internal`, `Customer review`, `Approved`, `Restricted`, `Unknown`.

Keep them on separate lines.

## Numbers

A result without a baseline is a vibe.

Record definition, baseline or comparison, result, period, population, calculation, attribution limit, source.

**Fails.** "Customers cut delivery delays by 28%."

**Passes.** "In a three-customer pilot, unresolved handoffs fell 28% within 60 days. Directional. Not a guarantee. Named use not approved."

Do not turn correlation or a compliment into causality.

## Quotes

Exact wording only. Speaker, date, context, locator. Paraphrases live outside quotation marks. Never rebuild a quote from a summary.

## Transfer

Keep the conditions that limit reuse: segment, implementation shape, what else changed. A story that hides constraints will be over-applied.

## Smallest branch

Quote bank, proof-point packet, internal narrative, customer-review draft, or publication packet. Do not produce the publication dossier for an internal proof request.

## Optional fields

Use status, identity mode, challenge, approach, outcomes table, quote bank, permission matrix, gaps that block the requested use.

# KPI Tree Examples

Use these as canonical examples when designing initiative-specific KPI trees.

## Example A: Onboarding Program

### Outcome layer
- New-hire time-to-first-productive-quarter
- Ramp attainment at day 90

### Behavior layer
- Discovery quality score trend
- Talk-track adherence in live calls
- Manager coaching consistency

### Activity layer
- Onboarding module completion
- Certification pass rate
- Practice session completion

### Interpretation caveats
- Segment new hires by role and experience level before comparing performance.
- Do not interpret completion rate alone as readiness quality.

## Example B: Product Launch Enablement

### Outcome layer
- Win rate movement on launch-impacted deals
- Cycle-time movement for launch-related opportunities

### Behavior layer
- Launch-message adoption in calls and follow-up notes
- Correct qualification and use-case mapping for new capability
- Manager inspection coverage in first 30 days

### Activity layer
- Launch training attendance
- Seller update digest consumption
- Roleplay or certification completion on launch scenario

### Interpretation caveats
- Separate launch impact from seasonality and pricing changes.
- Use confidence labels when sample size is still small.

## Minimal Decision Mapping

For each metric in your output, add:
- `continue` threshold
- `adjust` threshold
- `stop` or `deprioritize` threshold

This keeps the metrics tied to operating decisions rather than passive reporting.

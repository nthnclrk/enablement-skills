# Coaching cadence judgment

Use this when you are designing or checking a manager rhythm. The skill body has the procedure.

## From feedback to application

Each session needs an observed example, a specific change to practise, and an opportunity to apply it. Ask the rep to explain their intent before giving feedback. Rehearsal shows whether they understand the change; the next eligible work sample shows whether they can use it.

**Fails.** "Weekly 45-minute 1:1. Cover forecast, stuck deals, activity, and any coaching needs."

**Passes.** "Review the close of a discovery call, practise asking for a mutual next step, and listen for buyer agreement on the next comparable call. Check the agreed action, owner, and date."

## Capacity before coverage

```text
available individual time = protected minutes - group work - shared preparation - administration
complete individual session = per-rep evidence review + conversation and rehearsal + follow-up
sustainable individual sessions = floor(max(0, available individual time) / complete individual session)
```

Use positive session durations and one time unit. Count each activity once: shared preparation and per-rep preparation are different. If fixed work already exceeds protected time, report the overload before allocating sessions. State the evidence sample and time assumptions. If capacity does not cover the team:

1. Prioritize individual time by current, reviewable behavior evidence
2. Use group time only for a genuinely shared gap
3. Rotate the rest on a visible schedule
4. Name the shortfall. Do not delete review or follow-up to make the math work

New weekdays and deadlines are proposals until the manager confirms them.

**Fails.** "Coach all eight AEs weekly" inside 4.5 hours, with no review time shown.

**Passes.** "Proposed 270-minute budget: 30 minutes group practice, 15 shared preparation, 15 administration, and six 35-minute individual cycles. Each cycle includes 10 minutes evidence review, 20 minutes feedback and rehearsal, and 5 minutes follow-up. Two of eight AEs rotate to the next cycle. The manager must confirm these time estimates."

## Fit the rhythm to the work

- SMB velocity: short practice and frequent samples may fit discovery, objections, and next steps.
- Mid-market: use call or deal evidence for the behaviour being coached.
- Enterprise: allow for less frequent opportunities to practise stakeholder and decision-process work.
- Post-sale: renewal risk, expansion qualification, handoff quality.

Check actual opportunity volume rather than deriving frequency from the segment label alone.

## Thin evidence

If you do not yet know the behaviors or the evidence is unusable, return an observation-baseline: owner, sample, review point, and the decision needed before cadence design. Do not ship a full rhythm on a hypothesis.

Automated scores are leads for human review, not findings. One interaction is not a performance decision. The rep sees the evidence, adds context, and can correct facts.

Do not invent a number of missed commitments or coached cycles as an escalation rule. Use persistence, breadth, consequence, and evidence sufficiency. Name who must set a numeric cutoff.

**Fails.** "Escalate after three missed commitments" with no policy owner.

**Passes.** "If the gap persists across comparable opportunities despite practice and support, the manager reviews its consequences and possible workflow barriers with the People partner under the existing policy. A formal escalation threshold has not been supplied."

## Employee-data boundary

Capture the minimum role-relevant evidence. Do not copy health, family, or protected-characteristic information into coaching notes. Keep developmental records out of formal performance files unless an authorized process requires the move.

## Optional shape

```text
Capacity (minutes, coverage, who rotates)
Coaching focus (one behaviour, success signal, opportunity to apply)
Session: evidence / rep reflection / feedback / rehearsal / application
Observation fields
Commitment fields (owner, date, proof)
Trend review (skill vs process vs pipeline)
Escalation condition (owner of any numeric rule)
```

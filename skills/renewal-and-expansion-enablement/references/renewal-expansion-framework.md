# Renewal And Expansion Framework

Use this framework to keep post-sale revenue plays consistent across teams.

## Lifecycle Windows

- `120-90 days to renewal`: baseline health and stakeholder map validation
- `90-60 days`: renewal plan confirmation and risk mitigation
- `60-30 days`: commercial alignment and escalation path
- `30-0 days`: close plan execution and contingency path

## Renewal Risk Indicators

- declining product adoption against account baseline
- unresolved executive-level outcome commitments
- sponsor turnover with weak relationship coverage
- unresolved support or implementation blockers
- late-stage commercial friction without executive alignment

## Usage Signal Layer

Track at least:

- active-user trend vs account baseline
- feature adoption trend for core use cases
- usage concentration risk (single team/champion dependency)
- sustained value-metric movement tied to customer goals

## Expansion Readiness Indicators

- clear value realization in current scope
- multi-threaded stakeholder relationships
- adjacent use case with measurable business upside
- implementation capacity and timing fit

## Commercial Governance States

Define explicit owners and transitions for:

- pricing/discount approval
- procurement status
- legal/security review
- deal desk review
- executive escalation

## Play Matrix Minimum

Include at least:

- standard renewal play
- at-risk renewal play
- expansion discovery play
- expansion close play

For each play, define trigger, owner, sequence, and inspection checkpoint.

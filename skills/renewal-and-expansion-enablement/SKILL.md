# Renewal And Expansion Enablement

Create repeatable post-sale plays that connect adoption, value realization, and growth outcomes.

## Confirm Inputs First

Confirm these minimum inputs:

- Use supplied current context or relevant sections of `.enablement/context.md` when available. Treat stale, inferred, or conflicted material as provisional; never update shared context silently.
- Business model and contract mechanics: term end, renewal/termination notice, auto-renewal, pricing notice, and amendment rules
- Target segments and ownership model (CSM, AM, hybrid)
- Current renewal and expansion performance baseline
- Verified procurement, legal, security, budget, approval, and signature lead times
- Available product-usage, adoption, value, stakeholder, support, and commercial signals with definitions and as-of dates
- Commercial governance states (legal, procurement, pricing approvals, deal desk)
- Common risk patterns and expansion blockers
- Desired reporting horizon (quarterly, half-year, annual)

If critical items are missing, ask for the smallest missing set. Use labeled assumptions only for the internal workplan; do not invent contractual dates, notice periods, operational lead times, account signals, health thresholds, or expansion-readiness thresholds.

## Read The Right Reference

- Read [references/renewal-expansion-framework.md](references/renewal-expansion-framework.md) before defining lifecycle plays, qualification, and manager checkpoints.

## Default Workflow

1. Define the renewal and expansion windows by segment.
Derive each account timeline from contract dates, required notice, verified internal/buyer lead times, and budget or approval events. Mark unknown dates as blockers rather than applying a generic countdown.

2. Separate save motions from growth motions.
Define the signals that indicate retention risk versus whitespace or expansion potential.

3. Build the signal map.
Define each signal's formula or observation, source, account baseline, window, freshness, threshold owner, and unknown state.

4. Design the plays.
Write specific plays for healthy renewals, at-risk renewals, and expansion opportunities.

5. Add talk tracks and next actions.
Give CS, AM, Sales, and Support a clear playbook for what to say and do next.

6. Add commercial guardrails.
Define discount authority, deal desk handoffs, legal/procurement states, and exception rules.

7. Define manager inspection points.
Align review prompts and escalation triggers to contractual milestones, signal changes, and the team's operating cadence.

8. Operationalize the cadence.
Lock ownership, reporting cadence, and handoff rules into the team workflow.

## Tool Notes

- Tool-agnostic default: run from account plans, renewal lists, and meeting cadences.
- Optional tools: CRM, CS platforms, and product analytics can automate risk and opportunity flags.
- Keep logic portable so teams with low tooling maturity can still execute.
- Treat system health scores as inputs, not ground truth; preserve the component signals and their freshness.
- Do not update renewal dates, health states, expansion stages, or commercial fields without explicit user approval.

## Output Contract

Default output includes:

- Renewal and expansion play matrix by segment
- Contract-derived milestone map with source, owner, latest safe start, and unresolved gaps
- Risk and expansion trigger map
- Product-usage and adoption signal map
- Commercial governance-state map with owner by state
- Role-based talk tracks and action prompts
- Manager inspection checklist and cadence
- KPI set with definitions, baselines, and owner-approved thresholds

## Quality Bar

- Plays are tied to measurable account signals, not generic account management advice.
- Timelines are contract-derived and show the source and owner for each milestone.
- Every trigger has a defined positive, caution, risk, or unknown state; missing data is never treated as healthy.
- Roles, handoffs, and escalation points are explicit.
- Inspection criteria are concrete and repeatable.
- Recommendations reflect real commercial constraints.
- Usage and adoption signals are translated into specific plays.
- Output identifies rollout blockers and is immediately usable once required dates, thresholds, and owners are verified.

# Launch Readiness Framework

## 1) Launch Tiering

Classify launch impact:

- Tier 1: Minimal field impact
- Tier 2: Moderate process or narrative change
- Tier 3: Major motion, packaging, or positioning change

Higher tiers require deeper training, inspection, and readiness gates.

## 2) Readiness Dimensions

Assess readiness across:

- Message clarity
- Process clarity
- Objection readiness
- Operational support readiness
- Manager inspection readiness

## 3) Readiness Gates

Define gate criteria before go-live:

- Content complete and approved
- Training delivered to target audience
- Manager coaching prompts distributed
- Support escalation path documented

## 3a) Cross-Functional Owner Matrix

Minimum owner rows:

- PMM owner for narrative and positioning updates
- Enablement owner for training and reinforcement
- Sales/CS leadership owners for field execution
- RevOps owner for process/reporting updates
- Support owner for escalation path and FAQ handling

## 4) Early-Window Reinforcement

During first 2-4 weeks:

- Run weekly issue capture
- Publish rapid FAQ updates
- Coach against live examples
- Track adoption and friction indicators

## 5) 30-Day Signal Review Loop

- Run weekly adoption/failure-signal review in the first 30 days.
- Track message drift, objection spikes, and process confusion by role.
- Convert findings into prioritized updates with owner and due date.

# Discovery And Demo Framework

Use this framework to design conversations that produce decision clarity.

## Discovery Question Ladder

### Layer 1: Context

- What changed that made this priority now?
- Which team outcomes are currently at risk?

### Layer 2: Problem Depth

- Where does current process break down most?
- What downstream impact does that create?

### Layer 3: Business Impact

- What is the measurable cost of staying in current state?
- Which strategic goals are affected if this stays unresolved?

### Layer 4: Stakeholder And Decision Path

- Who must agree before a decision can be made?
- What evaluation criteria must be satisfied?

### Layer 5: Commitment And Next Step

- What must be true for the next meeting to be valuable?
- What is the agreed next decision checkpoint?

## Demo Narrative Pattern

- `Anchor`: restate desired business outcome.
- `Show`: demonstrate only the capabilities tied to that outcome.
- `Prove`: add evidence, benchmark, or customer example.
- `Handle Risk`: address likely adoption or implementation concerns.
- `Commit`: confirm next decision step with owner and date.

## Landmines To Avoid

- Running a feature tour before validating discovery outcomes.
- Demonstrating capabilities with no stakeholder-specific framing.
- Ending without explicit next-step ownership and timing.

# Discovery and demo judgment

Use this when designing or checking a meeting architecture. The skill body has the procedure.

## Demo gate

Include a demo block only when you have a verified buyer outcome, concern, or decision criterion, a matching approved capability, and a stop condition.

**Fails.** "Start with the platform overview, then walk all six modules."

**Passes.** "You said late handoffs hit the Friday ship. Show missed-window alert and owner assignment. Stop if that is not the belief they need."

If discovery does not support a block, remove it. Combined meetings reserve time to validate context before choosing what to show.

## Modes

- Discovery only: decision context, impact, stakeholders, criteria, next validation
- Demo only: confirm prior discovery, show selected proof, test fit
- Combined: validate, then demonstrate only what discovery unlocked

## Topic pattern

For each priority topic: reason, opening, listen-for, deepen, if/then branch, capture field.

Do not ask every prompt. Move on when the uncertainty is resolved.

## Decision areas

Change, current state, impact, desired state, stakeholders, criteria, process. Treat unknown authority, timing, and impact measures as questions.

## Close

Recap confirmed outcomes and open questions. Ask for a next step. Do not write a proposed action as an agreed commitment.

## Thin evidence

If you do not know the decision this meeting can produce, propose one and stop. Do not fill the hour with product.

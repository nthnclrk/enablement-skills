# Forecast Risk Framework

Use this framework for portfolio-level forecast reliability analysis.

## Risk Tier Criteria

### Low Risk

- Strong recent deal movement.
- Clear decision path and engaged stakeholders.
- Time-bound next steps with buyer ownership.

### Moderate Risk

- Some progression evidence, but one or more critical gaps.
- Partial stakeholder coverage or timeline ambiguity.
- Commercial pressure present but manageable.

### High Risk

- Weak progression evidence or prolonged stage aging.
- Unclear decision path or missing critical buyer roles.
- Slippage signals or unresolved commercial blockers.

## Core Risk Signals

- Stage aging vs normal cycle baseline.
- Next-step quality and owner clarity.
- Stakeholder depth and executive access.
- Sequence breaks in evaluation or procurement process.
- Late-period deal bunching and concentration risk.

## Confidence Band Guide

- `High Confidence`: low uncertainty, limited structural risk.
- `Medium Confidence`: some uncertainty, manageable risk concentration.
- `Low Confidence`: significant uncertainty or concentrated downside risk.

## Intervention Horizon

- `Immediate (this week)`: rescue critical commits and unblock buying process.
- `Near-Term (2-4 weeks)`: tighten qualification and progression checks.
- `Structural (next quarter)`: improve stage definitions and inspection cadence.

# Value Prop And Positioning

Build differentiated positioning and persona-level value messaging that sales and marketing teams can actually reuse.

## Confirm Inputs First

- Use supplied current context or relevant sections of `.enablement/context.md` when available. Treat stale, inferred, or conflicted material as provisional; never update shared context silently.
- Confirm the product or feature scope.
- Confirm ICP and target segment.
- Confirm the buyer task, triggering situation, success criteria, and cost of the current approach.
- Confirm primary personas, buying roles, and decision criteria.
- Confirm alternatives buyers actually use or consider, including no change, manual work, or in-house approaches.
- Confirm differentiated capabilities, how they produce an outcome, and available proof.
- Confirm claim permissions, source freshness, and restricted evidence.
- Confirm the primary commercial motion (new logo, expansion, enterprise, SMB).
- If inputs are incomplete, ask for only the minimum missing items or proceed with labeled hypotheses. Do not turn an unverified hypothesis into a competitive or outcome claim.

## Read The Right Reference

- Read [references/positioning-canvas.md](references/positioning-canvas.md) when you need a stable framework for framing alternatives, value pillars, and proof.

## Default Workflow

1. Define the buyer task.
Capture the triggering situation, progress sought, current approach, obstacles, decision criteria, and evidence of consequence.
2. Map real alternatives.
Use buyer, deal, research, or usage evidence to show what the buyer would do instead and why each option remains attractive.
3. Build the differentiation chain.
Connect a relevant differentiated capability to its mechanism, buyer outcome, and evidence; remove claims that are only different in wording.
4. Build the proof ledger.
Record exact claim language, source, population, timeframe, freshness, audience permission, owner, and permitted use.
5. Choose the thesis mode and write only the message pillars it supports: evidence-backed positioning, product-demonstrable mechanism language without an outcome claim, or an internal hypothesis with explicit validation needs and no external use.
6. Translate by decision role.
Change the task, decision criterion, outcome, proof, and risk—not only the vocabulary—for each role.
7. Add a why-now narrative only when a real trigger, cost of delay, or decision window exists.
8. State disqualification conditions and unsupported claims explicitly.
9. Route adjacent work:
   For call-level talk tracks, use talk-track-builder.
   For objection handling, use objection-library-builder.
   For competitor-specific guidance, use battlecard-builder.

## Output Contract

- Positioning thesis and category framing, labeled as evidence-backed, product-demonstrable, or internal hypothesis.
- Buyer-task and decision-criteria summary.
- Alternatives map of buyer-verified options, including no change when relevant.
- Differentiation chain: capability, mechanism, outcome, and evidence.
- Decision-role value matrix with task, criteria, outcome, proof, and risk.
- Why-now narrative and disqualification conditions.
- Message hierarchy: headline, pillar statements, and support points.
- Proof ledger with claim status, provenance, freshness, and audience permission.
- Assumptions list when source inputs were incomplete.

## What To Avoid

- Treating positioning as slogan copy instead of decision support.
- Reusing one message across all personas with only cosmetic edits.
- Leading with claims that have no defensible proof path.
- Presenting a product-demonstrable mechanism or internal hypothesis as a proven customer outcome.
- Treating an internal feature list as buyer differentiation.
- Manufacturing urgency when no credible trigger or cost of delay exists.

## Quality Bar

- Positioning is differentiated against real alternatives, not generic claims.
- Each value statement links buyer task, differentiated capability, mechanism, and outcome.
- Every material claim appears in the proof ledger and stays within its evidence and permission boundary.
- Persona messaging changes meaningfully by role.
- Output is concise enough for enablement reuse without rewriting.

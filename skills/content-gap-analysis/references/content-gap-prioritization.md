# Content Gap Prioritization Guide

Use this guide to score and sequence enablement content gaps.

## Coverage Dimensions

- Role: AE, SDR, SE, CSM, manager, partner.
- Stage: discovery, evaluation, commercial, onboarding, renewal.
- Job-to-be-done: message clarity, objection handling, technical proof, deal progression.

## Gap Scoring

Score value on three anchored 1-5 dimensions. Keep evidence confidence and effort separate.

### Business Impact

| Score | Anchor |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Convenience issue with no demonstrated performance effect |
| 2 | Local friction with limited stage, role, or customer consequence |
| 3 | Repeated friction plausibly affecting one important behavior or KPI |
| 4 | Material risk to conversion, cycle time, readiness, retention, or seller execution |
| 5 | Blocks a critical motion, deadline, compliance obligation, or high-value customer decision |

### Frequency And Reach

| Score | Anchor |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Isolated or rare case |
| 2 | Occasional issue in one team, role, or segment |
| 3 | Recurring issue in one material workflow or audience |
| 4 | Frequent issue across multiple teams, roles, or stages |
| 5 | Pervasive issue affecting most of the in-scope motion |

### Strategic Alignment And Urgency

| Score | Anchor |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | No active priority or decision depends on the gap |
| 2 | Indirect support for a lower-priority initiative |
| 3 | Supports an active team or quarterly priority |
| 4 | Directly tied to a committed initiative, launch, or executive objective |
| 5 | Time-critical launch, regulatory, contractual, or executive commitment |

Keep all three ratings visible. Do not calculate a default composite score: a weighting silently encodes a business tradeoff that the evidence does not establish. If the organization already has a scoring model, record its owner, version, and weights before using it. If the user wants a new weighted model, present it as a proposal for confirmation and show how the ordering changes under reasonable alternatives.

Never divide value by effort; that causes easy, low-value work to outrank important work.

## Evidence Confidence

| Grade | Anchor |
| --- | --- |
| Low | Single anecdote, unverified request, stale example, or unknown source independence |
| Medium | Repeated current signals, but coverage is limited or evidence comes mainly from one source class |
| High | Current, sufficiently representative evidence converges across independent qualitative and usage/findability sources |

Confidence is a decision input, not a value multiplier. Assign a tier with explicit judgment rather than universal numeric cutoffs:

- `Mandatory`: a confirmed regulatory, contractual, safety, or committed launch obligation requires action. Show the governing source and deadline.
- `Commit now`: the gap and root cause are sufficiently supported, the consequence is material or blocking, and the work serves a current priority or time-bound decision.
- `Validate first`: the potential consequence is material, but the root cause, reach, evidence independence, or user need remains uncertain. Name the smallest validation that would change the decision.
- `Plan`: the gap is supported and worthwhile, but no immediate obligation or critical dependency justifies displacing higher-priority work.
- `Monitor`: the signal is isolated, low-consequence, or not yet distinguishable from discoverability, distribution, process, or data-quality friction.

High severity can justify immediate containment or investigation even when frequency is low. High frequency does not by itself prove high business impact.

Estimate effort as `S/M/L` or person-days and list dependencies. Use effort only to sequence work within a decision tier, alongside deadline and owner capacity.

## Action Types

- `Create`: no fit-for-purpose asset exists and evidence confirms the job.
- `Update`: the authoritative asset exists but is stale, inaccurate, incomplete, or in the wrong format.
- `Consolidate`: overlapping assets create conflict or search friction; preserve one owner and canonical location.
- `Improve discoverability`: the asset is fit for purpose but taxonomy, search, navigation, or permissions prevent access.
- `Reinforce distribution`: the asset is findable and fit for purpose but the intended audience has not adopted it.
- `Retire`: the asset is invalid, superseded, redundant, or no longer strategically relevant, and the authorized owner approves retirement.

Low usage alone never proves that an asset should be retired.

# Content Gap Prioritization Guide

Use this guide to score and sequence enablement content gaps.

## Coverage Dimensions

- Role: AE, SDR, SE, CSM, manager, partner.
- Stage: discovery, evaluation, commercial, onboarding, renewal.
- Job-to-be-done: message clarity, objection handling, technical proof, deal progression.

## Gap Scoring

Score each gap on a 1-5 scale:

- `Impact`: expected effect on win rate, cycle time, conversion, or retention.
- `Frequency`: how often the gap appears in real workflows.
- `Confidence`: quality of supporting evidence.
- `Effort`: relative creation or update effort.

Suggested priority formula:

`Priority Score = (Impact * Frequency * Confidence) / Effort`

## Action Types

- `Create`: no usable asset exists.
- `Update`: asset exists but is outdated or weak.
- `Consolidate`: duplicates create confusion.
- `Retire`: low usage or no strategic relevance.

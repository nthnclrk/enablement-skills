Use this when the brief depends on live account data rather than a manually assembled summary.

# Source System Guide

## Useful Sources

- CRM: account hierarchy, open opportunities, closed history, recent activity, contacts, renewal dates.
- Conversation intelligence: stakeholder sentiment, objections, commitments, and recent call themes.
- Internal docs / notes: strategy memos, meeting notes, mutual action plans, product escalations.

## Minimum Pull

- Account name, segment, commercial state, current opportunities or renewal status.
- Top stakeholders and their apparent stance.
- Last 30-90 days of meaningful activity.
- Known risks, blockers, and next-step commitments.

## Recommended Handling

- Prefer CRM for current commercial state and contact records.
- Use calls and notes to add stakeholder nuance, not to replace source-of-truth fields.
- Add a visible freshness note when sources are older than the decision window.

## Safe Writeback Boundary

- Do not overwrite account plans, CRM fields, or executive notes automatically.
- Deliver a reusable brief and let the operator decide which systems to update.

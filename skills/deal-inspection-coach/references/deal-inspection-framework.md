# Deal Inspection Framework

Use this framework to inspect one deal consistently.

## Qualification Blocks

- `Problem And Priority`: Is business pain explicit and prioritized?
- `Value Case`: Is expected business impact clear and credible?
- `Stakeholder Coverage`: Are buying roles mapped and engaged?
- `Decision Process`: Is process and approval path explicit?
- `Competition`: Is the alternative set known and actively addressed?
- `Execution Path`: Are milestones, owners, and timing concrete?

## Evidence Strength Rating

- `Strong`: objective proof from buyer action or confirmed data.
- `Moderate`: partial proof, inferred from consistent signals.
- `Weak`: assumed or unverified seller narrative.

## Risk Types

- `Qualification Risk`: missing critical buying information.
- `Process Risk`: unclear decision path or procurement unknowns.
- `Stakeholder Risk`: weak access, no champion, or blocker dominance.
- `Commercial Risk`: pricing pressure, value mismatch, or budget instability.
- `Timing Risk`: low urgency, slippage signals, or competing initiatives.

## Action Prioritization

- Prioritize actions by `impact x controllability x urgency`.
- Assign one owner and one due date per priority action.
- Define one checkpoint question to verify progress.

# Impact Story Template

## 1) Decision Context

- Audience
- Decision needed
- Time horizon

## 2) Initiatives In Scope

For each initiative:

- Objective
- Target behavior
- Rollout period

## 3) Evidence Summary

List:

- Key leading indicators
- Key lagging indicators
- Direction and magnitude of change

## 4) Contribution Narrative

Describe likely contribution chain:

1. Enablement action
2. Behavior change observed
3. Business outcome movement

Do not present this as guaranteed causality.

## 5) Assumptions And Confounders

- Assumptions used
- External factors that may influence outcomes
- Confidence level per claim

## 6) Recommended Next Actions

- Continue
- Adjust
- Stop

Include owner and expected impact for each recommendation.

## 7) Decisions To Make Now

For each decision:

- Decision statement
- Options considered
- Trade-offs and risk
- Recommended option
- Decision owner and due date
